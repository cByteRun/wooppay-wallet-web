# 💼 BUSINESS VISION — Wooppay Wallet Web

**🏷️ Product Category:** FinTech / B2C Growth / B2B Lead Generation / SEO & GEO

**🚀 Product Name:** Wooppay Wallet — the official web portal and organic user acquisition platform.

### 🎯 1. Product Objective

Create a unified official Wooppay Wallet web resource that builds trust with banks and partners, attracts users to the mobile application through organic search, and establishes the brand's presence in AI assistant responses.

📈 Transform the website from a simple informational page into a measurable channel for acquiring customers and B2B partners.

### ⚠️ 2. Business Problem

Wooppay Wallet lacks a unified official web platform, resulting in four key problems:

- 🤝 **B2B:** Banks and partners have to manually share presentations and explain the product, complicating initial negotiations.
- 📱 **B2C:** Potential users cannot easily find official information about fees, transfers, limits, and service payments.
- 📣 **Marketing:** Dependence on paid advertising and partner channels increases user acquisition costs.
- 🎧 **Customer Support:** Repetitive questions create additional operational workload.

### 🛠️ 3. Business Solution

Develop a multilingual, multi-page Wooppay Wallet website with four business modules:

1. 🤝 **B2B Partner Hub** — product presentation, trust-building materials, and an inquiry form for banks and partners.
2. 📲 **B2C Acquisition** — SEO landing pages for services with links to the App Store and Google Play.
3. 🔎 **SEO + GEO Engine** — structured content, FAQs, and guides optimized for search engines and citation by AI assistants.
4. 📚 **Self-Service Knowledge Base** — a knowledge base containing fees, limits, and instructions, managed through a CMS without developer involvement.

### 📊 4. Economic Impact

**🎯 Target KPIs for Month 6 after launch:**

| Area                                   | Expected Result                                |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 🌐 Organic Traffic                     | 10,000 visits/month                            |
| 📲 Conversion to App Store Visits      | 15% = 1,500 visits/month                       |
| 🤝 B2B Lead Generation                 | ≥5 partner inquiries/month                    |
| 🎧 Customer Support Workload Reduction | −20% of routine inquiries                     |
| 🔎 SEO                                 | Top 10 rankings for 3 priority search queries  |
| 🤖 GEO                                 | Measurable brand mentions in AI-powered search |

**💡 Business Impact:** Reduced dependence on paid traffic, lower customer support operating costs, and the development of an owned, scalable acquisition channel for customers and partners.

**💰 Financial Model:**

- 🧮 **Support Cost Savings** = Number of prevented inquiries × Cost per inquiry handled.
- 📈 **Organic Acquisition Impact** = Number of verified app installs × Comparable paid acquisition cost.
- 🤝 **B2B Value** = Number of qualified leads × Conversion rate to contracts × Average profit per partner.

💵 Monetary ROI will be calculated once actual conversion, cost, and revenue data become available.

### 🧪 5. Key Business Hypothesis

**A unified official website with SEO/GEO optimization will enable Wooppay Wallet to simultaneously increase organic user acquisition, accelerate engagement with B2B partners, and reduce customer support costs without a proportional increase in marketing expenditure.**

### 📝 Important Notes on the Technical Specification

⚠️ The published assignment specifies a 20% reduction in support inquiries, while the attached technical document specifies 15%.

📉 The assignment also mentions 2,000 app installs per month from the website, although 10,000 visits × 15% result in only 1,500 visits to app stores.

✅ For this Business Vision, I used the 20% target from the assignment brief and the mathematically consistent figure of 1,500 app store visits. The actual number of app installs will need to be measured separately.
