
# 💼 BUSINESS VISION — Wooppay Wallet Web

**Official Digital Platform for B2C Acquisition, B2B Lead Generation & Self-Service**

**Product Category:** FinTech / B2C Growth / B2B Lead Generation / SEO & GEO

**Product:** Wooppay Wallet — an official web platform designed to increase customer acquisition, improve partner engagement, reduce repetitive support workload, and strengthen Wooppay’s digital presence.

**Planning Horizon:** 2026–2030
**Initial KPI Horizon:** 6 months after launch

---

## 🎯 1. Product Vision

Transform the Wooppay Wallet website from a primarily informational resource into a measurable business channel that supports customer acquisition, partner onboarding, and operational efficiency.

The platform will provide a unified digital entry point for consumers, merchants, banks, and technology partners by combining product information, service discovery, partner solutions, self-service documentation, and conversion analytics.

The website will support three business outcomes:

* **📈 Growth:** attract qualified organic traffic and convert visitors into app-store visits, verified installs, and active users.
* **🤝 B2B Revenue Enablement:** help potential partners understand Wooppay’s solutions and submit qualified partnership inquiries.
* **⚙️ Operational Efficiency:** reduce repetitive support requests by providing accessible, structured, and searchable information.

The strategic objective is not simply to launch a modern website. It is to establish an owned, measurable digital channel that contributes to Wooppay’s long-term business growth.

## ⚠️ 3. Business Problem

The proposed project addresses four business challenges that should be validated against Wooppay’s internal analytics.

### 3.1 B2B partner acquisition

Potential partners may need to contact Wooppay to understand available payment solutions, integration options, commercial requirements, and onboarding steps.

Without a clear digital presentation of these capabilities, initial qualification can require additional manual communication.

**Business opportunity:** establish a Partner Hub with dedicated product pages, integration information, qualification criteria, and structured lead capture.

### 3.2 B2C discovery and conversion

Potential customers need clear answers about available services, payment conditions, limits, fees, and how to start using the wallet.

If information is difficult to discover through search engines or the official website, potential customers may abandon the journey before reaching the application.

**Business opportunity:** build search-optimized service pages with direct app-store links and measurable conversion events.

### 3.3 Repetitive customer support

Questions about fees, limits, payment instructions, service availability, and account procedures can create recurring support workload.

Wooppay publicly advertises round-the-clock support. The proposed website should complement this service, not replace it.

**Business opportunity:** provide a searchable knowledge base and structured FAQs that resolve suitable questions before a support request is submitted.

### 3.4 Digital trust and product communication

Wooppay’s public portfolio spans consumer services, online payment acceptance, and platform solutions. A unified information architecture can help each audience understand the relevant offering without confusing the wallet with the wider payment infrastructure.

**Business opportunity:** present product capabilities, security information, official documentation, and partner propositions through clearly differentiated pages.

## 🛠️ 4. Proposed Business Solution

Develop a multilingual, multi-page website with four integrated business modules.

### Module 1 — B2B Partner Hub

**Purpose:** increase qualified partner inquiries and reduce manual explanation during initial discussions.

Core functionality:

* Dedicated pages for relevant business solutions.
* Partner use cases and integration options.
* Clear onboarding requirements and process descriptions.
* Frequently asked commercial and technical questions.
* Structured partnership inquiry form.
* Lead-source tracking and conversion analytics.

**Business KPI:** at least 5 qualified partner inquiries per month by month 6.

### Module 2 — B2C Acquisition Engine

**Purpose:** convert organic search demand into measurable app acquisition.

Core functionality:

* SEO landing pages for payment services and wallet use cases.
* Clear product benefits and service descriptions.
* Direct App Store and Google Play links.
* Mobile-first responsive design.
* Analytics for store visits, installs where measurable, and downstream activation where available.

**Business KPI:** 10,000 organic visits per month and a 15% click-through rate to app stores by month 6.

### Module 3 — SEO & GEO Content Engine

**Purpose:** increase the discoverability of official Wooppay information in search engines and AI-assisted search experiences.

Core functionality:

* Search-intent-based landing pages.
* Structured FAQs and instructional content.
* Appropriate structured data markup.
* Clear source-of-truth pages for fees, limits, services, and payment procedures.
* Consistent product terminology across the website.
* Monitoring of organic visibility and attributable referrals from AI-powered search where measurable.

**Business KPI:** top-10 rankings for three priority search queries and measurable AI-search brand mentions.

AI-search mentions are an exploratory visibility metric, not a guaranteed source of traffic or revenue.

### Module 4 — Self-Service Knowledge Base

**Purpose:** reduce repetitive support workload and make official product information easier to maintain.

Core functionality:

* Searchable FAQs.
* Fees, limits, and service conditions.
* Step-by-step payment instructions.
* Account and security guidance.
* Content management through a CMS without developer involvement for routine updates.
* Analytics for searches, article engagement, and support deflection.

**Business KPI:** reduce eligible routine support inquiries by 20% relative to a measured baseline.

## 💰 6. Economic Impact Model

The project has three potential economic value streams:

1. Lower support workload.
2. Reduced dependence on paid acquisition.
3. Additional partner opportunities.

These effects must be measured independently and consolidated without double-counting.

### 6.1 Support time savings

Use the following model:

**Hours saved per month = Monthly eligible inquiries × Deflection rate × Average handling time / 60**

The original assignment specifies a 20% reduction in routine inquiries. Because Wooppay has not publicly disclosed the relevant ticket volume or average handling time, the following scenarios are illustrative rather than company forecasts.

| Scenario      | Eligible inquiries/month | Average handling time | Deflection target | Hours saved/month | Hours saved/year |
| ------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| Low-volume    | 500                      | 6 min                 | 20%               | 10                | 120              |
| Base scenario | 1,000                    | 8 min                 | 20%               | 26.7              | 320              |
| High-volume   | 2,000                    | 10 min                | 20%               | 66.7              | 800              |

The model indicates a potential annual capacity release of  **120–800 support hours** , depending on actual inquiry volume and handling time.

The base scenario represents approximately 320 hours per year, equivalent to 40 eight-hour working days.

This is released capacity, not automatically a reduction in payroll or headcount.

### 6.2 Illustrative support cost impact

**Economic value of released capacity = Hours saved × Fully loaded hourly labor cost**

Using an illustrative labor-cost range of ₸3,000–₸5,000 per hour:

| Scenario      | Hours saved/month | Monthly capacity value | Annual capacity value    |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Low-volume    | 10                | ₸30,000–₸50,000     | ₸360,000–₸600,000     |
| Base scenario | 26.7              | ₸80,000–₸133,000    | ₸960,000–₸1.6 million |
| High-volume   | 66.7              | ₸200,000–₸333,000   | ₸2.4–₸4.0 million     |

These estimates are sensitivity scenarios, not verified Wooppay cost savings.

Real cash savings arise only if the released capacity reduces overtime, external support expenditure, hiring needs, or another actual cost. Otherwise, the benefit is increased operational capacity.

### 6.3 Organic acquisition value

The proposed website targets 10,000 organic visits per month.

At a 15% click-through rate, this produces 1,500 app-store visits per month.

For a preliminary comparison, assume a paid traffic equivalent of ₸100–₸300 per visit:

**Equivalent paid media value = Organic visits × Comparable paid cost per visit**

| Metric                       | Illustrative result |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Organic visits/month         | 10,000              |
| Assumed comparable paid cost | ₸100–₸300        |
| Equivalent media value/month | ₸1–₨3 million    |
| Equivalent media value/year  | ₸12–₨36 million  |

The currency notation above should be interpreted in tenge: **₸1–₸3 million per month and ₸12–₸36 million per year.**

This represents an illustrative replacement value of organic traffic, not net profit or guaranteed cash savings. The actual financial benefit depends on paid acquisition benchmarks, SEO costs, traffic quality, attribution, and conversion into valuable users.

### 6.4 B2B lead generation value

The target of five qualified partner inquiries per month corresponds to 60 inquiries per year if the target is sustained.

**Expected first-year partner profit = Annual qualified leads × Contract conversion rate × Average first-year profit per partner**

Illustrative scenario:

* 60 qualified leads per year.
* 10% conversion to a signed contract.
* ₸2 million average first-year profit per acquired partner.

Result:

**60 × 10% × ₸2,000,000 = ₸12 million**

This is a scenario, not a Wooppay revenue forecast. Actual results depend on lead quality, sales-cycle duration, contract conversion, implementation costs, and partner profitability.

## 🧮 7. Consolidated Value Framework

The website’s financial case should distinguish between directly realized savings, acquisition value, and potential partner profit.

| Value stream        | Primary metric                                          | Financial treatment                                                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Support efficiency  | Hours released and actual cost avoided                  | Count realized cost savings; report released capacity separately            |
| Organic acquisition | Attributable incremental users and equivalent paid cost | Subtract SEO, content, and maintenance costs                                |
| B2B acquisition     | Incremental signed contracts and partner contribution   | Count attributable contribution profit, not gross lead value                |
| GEO visibility      | Mentions and attributable referrals                     | Treat as an experimental leading indicator until conversion is demonstrated |

**ROI formula:**

ROI = (Measured financial benefit − Total project cost) / Total project cost × 100%

Total project cost should include initial design and development, CMS implementation, analytics, content production, SEO, localization, maintenance, and ongoing optimization.

No defensible total ROI can be stated until project costs and Wooppay’s internal operating data are available.

## 🔐 8. Trust, Security & Compliance Communication

Wooppay’s public materials emphasize payment security, regulated electronic-money operations, and technical infrastructure.

The website should communicate these areas through:

* Official product and company information.
* Clearly explained payment and account procedures.
* Accurate security and authentication descriptions.
* Links to relevant official policies and documentation.
* Transparent fees and service conditions.
* Partner and integration information supported by approved evidence.

The July 2026 Visa Payment Passkey announcement provides a concrete example of Wooppay’s payment-technology development. The website can use approved public information about such initiatives to strengthen product credibility without implying that every wallet operation uses the same authentication method.

## 🧪 10. Key Business Hypothesis

**A unified official Wooppay Wallet web platform, combining B2C acquisition, B2B partner enablement, SEO/GEO content, and self-service documentation, can create a measurable and scalable digital acquisition channel while reducing repetitive support workload.**

The hypothesis will be validated through organic acquisition, qualified partner inquiries, support deflection, verified app installs, and financial attribution.

## 📌 11. Data Required to Validate the Business Case

Before finalizing the investment decision, obtain the following internal baselines:

1. Monthly support inquiries by category, average handling time, and fully loaded hourly cost.
2. Current organic traffic, paid acquisition costs, and available app-install attribution.
3. Current B2B inquiry volume, lead qualification rate, contract conversion, and partner contribution margin.
4. Website development and maintenance costs.
5. Baseline SEO performance and the measurement methodology for AI-search visibility.

These inputs will convert the illustrative scenarios into a company-specific financial model.

## Final Business Outcome

The proposed website is intended to become a measurable business asset rather than a standalone marketing deliverable.

Its success should be evaluated through three outcomes:

* **Growth:** qualified organic traffic, verified app installs, and B2B opportunities.
* **Efficiency:** fewer repetitive inquiries, released employee hours, and demonstrable cost avoidance.
* **Long-term value:** a maintainable official source of product information that supports Wooppay’s consumer, merchant, and infrastructure businesses through 2030.
